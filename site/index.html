What goes where

DeltaRoute connects the AI subscriptions you already pay for. Claude keeps the job and hands off only the parts another AI clearly does best: live research with sources to Perplexity, Grok and Muse, and images and video to Nano Banana Pro, ChatGPT, Veo and others. Whatever comes back, Claude checks before you see it.

Who counts as better comes from a scoreboard built from public benchmarks. Once a week DeltaRoute reads the latest results and updates the scores in small steps, each one cited and easy to undo. Claude keeps a job unless another AI scores at least 5 points higher.

Who it’s for

  • You pay for Claude and at least one other AI, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Grok.
  • You want research with real sources, images or video without leaving your Claude chat.
  • You take on big projects, like apps, websites or videos, and want them planned, split up and reviewed for you.
  • You’re on Windows. Mac and Linux work, but take more manual setup.

DeltaRoute doesn’t come with any AI accounts or credits. It only uses the apps you already have, signed in the normal way.

Install it, no coding needed

  1. Install the free helpers

    Download and run the installers for Node.js (LTS), Git and Python. Clicking Next through each one is fine. Also install Claude with Claude Code.

  2. Download and unzip

    Click Download for Windows above. Right-click the ZIP, choose Extract All, and put the folder somewhere it can stay, like Documents.

  3. Let Claude set it up

    Open Claude Code, choose the DeltaRoute folder you just unzipped, and paste this:

    Set up DeltaRoute in this folder: run setup.ps1, then install.ps1 -AllowRouterTools, then doctor.ps1, and tell me in plain words if anything needs my attention.

  4. Sign in to your other AIs

    Sign in to the apps you want to use (like Codex or Gemini) the usual way, then restart Claude. That’s it.

How to use it

Just ask

Talk to Claude like normal. When part of the job needs live research or an image, Claude hands it to the AI that’s best at it. You can also name one: “make this with Nano Banana Pro.”

Approve the plan

For a big job, say “use DeltaRoute”. Claude shows a step-by-step plan and which AI does each part. Nothing starts until you say yes.

Watch it come together

Follow along on a live dashboard. The AIs share a team board and can ask each other for help. Each step is scored out of 100 and fixed if it lands under 80, and you get the final say.

Use it from your phone or ChatGPT

Ask Claude to run connect.ps1. It needs Tailscale (free) and prints a private link once. Add that link as a custom connector in the Claude app or ChatGPT. Keep it private, since anyone with it can use your subscriptions. It works while your PC is on.
